Word Burst Buddies

Rules for 2nd Graders
  • 1. Make words with the letters shown.
  • 2. Words must be 3 letters or more.
  • 3. Include the prompt letters somewhere in your word.
  • 4. Take turns typing your word.
  • 5. You have 3 lives. Lose a life if the word is wrong.
  • 6. Last person standing wins!

Have fun guys! - Davis G
